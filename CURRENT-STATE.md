# Current State

Last updated: 2026-11-24

Craig Gilmore's portfolio site (comic book creator, storyboard artist and illustrator) is being moved off WordPress, which is still live at https://www.craiggilmore.net. The replacement is a static Astro site hosted on Cloudflare Workers.

The rebuild and a full design pass are finished and merged to `main`. Open work is tracked in [TODO.md](TODO.md).

## Where things live

- **GitHub:** `macktropolis/craiggilmore_net` (renamed from `craig-gilmore-v2`; the old name redirects). Work happens on `main`.
- **Local working copy:** `~/Developer/craiggilmore_net`. Use this one for day-to-day work.
- **Node:** Astro needs Node 22.23.4 or newer. `.node-version` pins 22.

## Project layout

- **Config:** `astro.config.mjs` sets `site: 'https://www.craiggilmore.net'` and `trailingSlash: 'ignore'`.
- **Content collections** are defined in `src/content.config.ts`:
  - `src/content/portfolio/*.md` holds 68 projects. Frontmatter: title, credit, date, types (storyboards, concept-art or comics), tags, publisher (comics only), cover, videos and images.
  - `src/content/news/*.md` holds Craig's 7 real posts.
  - Both were generated by `scripts/import-wordpress.py`, a one-off importer. Don't re-run it casually: it overwrites the Markdown.
- **Pages** are in `src/pages/`:
  - Home, the full Portfolio, Storyboards, Concept Art, Comics, Ethereal (plus its signup thank-you page), About, News and 404.
  - Each project gets its own page at `/portfolio/<slug>/`.
  - Storyboards is hand-written. The other portfolio pages are built from the collection.
- **Data and helpers** are in `src/data/`:
  - `site.ts`: nav, footer nav, social links, the store URL and `BREVO_FORM_URL`.
  - `portfolio.ts`: `getProjects`, `projectUrl`, `sectionOf`, and the type, tag and publisher label maps.
  - `images.ts` with `thumbs.json`: `thumb()` maps an upload path to its thumbnail.
- **Components** are in `src/components/`: Header, Footer, PageBanner, ProjectGrid and Lightbox.
- **Styles:**
  - `src/styles/global.css`: design tokens, header, homepage and page banner.
  - `src/styles/gallery.css`: cards, galleries, filters and lightbox.
- **Scripts:**
  - `scripts/make-thumbs.py`: creates WebP thumbnails.
  - `scripts/make-icons.py`: creates the favicon and touch icons from Craig's logo.
  - `scripts/import-wordpress.py`: the one-off importer.

## Images

- **Originals:** `public/content/uploads/` holds the WordPress uploads, about 330 MB.
- **Thumbnails:** `public/content/thumbs/` holds an 800 px WebP copy of every upload the site references, named `<original name>.webp`, for example `foo.jpg.webp`.
  - Galleries and cards show the thumbnails, and the lightbox opens the originals.
  - This cut gallery pages by about 80%. Storyboards went from 12.4 MB to 1.9 MB.
  - After adding or changing images, run `python3 scripts/make-thumbs.py`. It skips thumbnails that are already up to date and rewrites `src/data/thumbs.json`.
- **Homepage art:** `public/images/home/` holds resized WebP files for:
  - the hero slides;
  - the portfolio cards;
  - the Ethereal cover;
  - Craig's portrait.
  - The slide list is the `slides` array at the top of `src/pages/index.astro`.

## Design system

- **Colours:** background `#090909`, crimson accent `#C8003E` (brighter version `#E8004A`). Text colours are tuned for readable contrast.
- **Fonts:** headings use Anton in capitals. Body text uses Barlow, and labels and nav use Barlow Condensed. All three load from Google Fonts.
- **Header:**
  - Transparent at the top of the page, turning into a frosted bar once you scroll.
  - The large logo hangs below the bar and shrinks into it on scroll.
  - "Portfolio" links to the full portfolio. Its dropdown lists Storyboards, Concept Art and Comics.
- **Homepage hero:** a crossfading slideshow of five pieces (Thor, Swamp Thing, Let's Be Cops, Cell and Rings).
  - It has dots and a pause button.
  - It pauses while the tab is in the background.
  - It doesn't play automatically for visitors with "reduce motion" turned on.
- **Inner pages:** each uses `PageBanner`, with a related piece of artwork fading in behind the right side. Project pages end with Previous and Next links.
- **Lightbox:**
  - Opens with the keyboard (Tab to an image, then Enter).
  - Has Previous and Next buttons and a counter.
  - Keeps Tab focus inside while open and returns focus to the image when closed.
- **Accessibility:**
  - Visible keyboard focus outlines everywhere.
  - Content that fades in on scroll is only hidden once JavaScript has run, via an `html.js` class.
  - "Reduce motion" is respected throughout.

## Gotchas

- **File-name case.** Some WordPress uploads differ only by upper or lower case. On a case-insensitive Mac disk they overwrite each other.
  - Twelve such files were restored as `*-alt.jpg` in `public/content/uploads/2013/02/`, with redirects in `_redirects`.
  - Don't re-download uploads by their original names.
  - When adding files, check the name doesn't clash with another one apart from case.
- **Spaces between lines.** Astro can drop the space when text ends a line and the next line starts with a tag. For example, `under` followed by `<a>` on the next line renders as "underintellectual". Keep the text and the inline tag on one line.
- **`getStaticPaths` scope.** It can't use functions defined in the page's own frontmatter. Put shared helpers in `src/data/`.
- **Header blur.** The header's `backdrop-filter` traps fixed-position children, which is why the mobile menu panel sits outside `<header>`.
